Tabline

Pick a screen. Keep the moment.

Capture. Copy. Save.

Press Alt + S, scroll the hanging cards and click one. The rope closes, opens your selected tab or app and copies a fresh screenshot. Hover for Copy or Save. Save opens a PNG destination dialog. Paste copied screenshots with Ctrl + V. Escape or a click outside closes the rope.

Previews stay simple.

A small tab thumbnail is updated once after visiting a tab, and after a screenshot is copied. No typing or scrolling tracker, continuous capture or full-size screenshot history. A missing thumbnail never prevents clicking a card.

App previews use Windows' built-in compositor while the rope is open. They are released on close. This version does not run Windows Graphics Capture or use Chrome's debugger.

Local and temporary.

No uploads or accounts. Only small last-seen images are held in session memory: up to 4 MiB for tabs and 1 MiB for apps. Full-resolution images are released after copying or saving; Windows retains your copied image on the clipboard. Only screenshots you explicitly save are written to disk.

Screenshot behavior.

Loaded tabs can be captured quickly. An unloaded tab must finish loading after activation. The current viewport is copied at its original resolution. Windows apps are restored and brought forward before capture; another always-on-top window or protected system surface may affect the screenshot. Success or failure appears in a small bottom-center notification.
