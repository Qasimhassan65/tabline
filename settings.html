Tabline

Pick a screen. Keep the moment.

Capture. Copy. Save.

Press Alt + S, scroll the hanging cards and click one. The rope closes and copies a fresh screenshot of your selected tab or app. Hover for Copy or Save. Save opens a PNG destination dialog. Paste copied screenshots with Ctrl + V. Escape or a click outside closes the rope.

Previews stay simple.

A small tab thumbnail is updated after visiting a tab, and after a screenshot is copied. No typing or scrolling tracker, continuous recording or full-size screenshot history. A missing thumbnail never prevents clicking a card.

App previews are provided by your platform’s companion.

Run companion\Install.cmd in the project folder with the extension ID above.

Mac testing edition · macOS 14 or later.

  1. Download the latest successful Mac development build from GitHub Actions. Choose the artifact matching your Mac: arm64 for Apple Silicon or x86_64 for Intel.
  2. Extract it and run Install.command. It installs the companion in your user Applications folder and opens setup.
  3. Paste the extension ID above into the companion’s setup window and connect.
  4. Allow Screen Recording in macOS System Settings. Restart Chrome if requested.
  5. Return here, check the connection, then press Option + S.

The test build is not notarized for public distribution. macOS may require approval under Privacy & Security. No companion is installed or permission granted silently.

Your shortcut.

Local and temporary.

No uploads or accounts for screenshots. Only small last-seen images are held in memory: up to 4 MiB for tabs and a bounded 1 MiB app preview cache, plus currently displayed images. Full-resolution capture buffers are released after copying or saving; your operating system retains the copied image on its clipboard. Only screenshots you explicitly save are written to disk.

Screenshot behavior.

Loaded Chrome tabs can be captured quickly; an unloaded tab must finish loading after activation. Windows apps are brought forward before capture. The Mac companion captures available app windows directly; some minimized windows need restoration. Protected surfaces can refuse capture. Success or failure appears in a bottom-center notification.
